# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of TradeOps, a general-purpose (not solar-specific) edition of the SolarOps codebase at SolarOps 1.0.6: blue identity, new ring-and-dot logo, new installer id, new license and recovery keys.
Inventory, purchase and sales orders, offers, returns, payments, cash boxes (USD/SYP), accounts, partners, branches and cars, WhatsApp, backups and licensing carry over unchanged.
